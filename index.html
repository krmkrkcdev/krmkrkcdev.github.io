Upload Planner

A small personal tool for uploading and scheduling videos on my own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads a video file from my computer to my YouTube channel.
  • Fills in the title, description, tags, category, language and audience settings.
  • Schedules the publish date and time.
  • Optionally sets a custom thumbnail and adds the video to a playlist.

Who uses it

Upload Planner is used only by its developer, for channels the developer owns or manages. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts and has no server: it runs as a command-line script on the developer's own computer and talks directly to the YouTube Data API.

How it works

The script signs in with Google OAuth, uploads the video straight from the local computer to YouTube, and stores the resulting sign-in token only on that computer. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Upload Planner uses YouTube API Services. By using Upload Planner, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
