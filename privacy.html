Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Upload Planner ("the app") is a personal command-line tool that uploads and schedules videos on its developer's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

  • Google account authorization. When you sign in, Google issues an OAuth token that lets the app act on your YouTube channel with these permissions: youtube.upload (upload videos) and youtube (set thumbnails, add videos to playlists and read back the details of videos the app uploaded).
  • Content you choose to upload. The video file, thumbnail and metadata you provide: title, description, tags, category, language, audience setting, privacy setting, publish time and playlist.
  • Upload results. The video ID and status that YouTube returns after an upload, so the app can show you a link to the video.

The app does not access your email messages, contacts, viewing history, subscribers, comments or analytics.

How the information is used

The information is used only to carry out the uploads you start: sending your video and its metadata to YouTube, scheduling it, and confirming the result. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and retention

  • The OAuth token is stored only on your own computer, in a local file named token.json. It stays there until you delete it or revoke access.
  • The app has no server and no database. Videos and metadata travel directly from your computer to YouTube; the app keeps no copies of them and does not store data obtained from the YouTube API.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone. The only recipient is Google/YouTube, as required to perform the upload.

Cookies and tracking

The app does not use cookies, analytics or any tracking technology, and does not place or read information on your device beyond the local token.json file described above. This website has no analytics either; it is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page.
  • Deleting the local token.json file removes the stored sign-in from your computer.
  • Videos you uploaded remain on YouTube until you delete them in YouTube Studio.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Upload Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
